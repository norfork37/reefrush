Privacy Policy

App: Reef Rush (iPhone and iPad)
Effective date: September 23, 2026
Contact: norfork37@gmail.com

In short: Reef Rush needs no account, and we do not run servers that collect your personal information. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between rounds. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure those ads, and you control ad personalization through the consent and tracking prompts. Optional Game Center and iCloud features are provided by Apple. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

Reef Rush is published by the developer listed on the App Store page (“we”, “us”). Write to norfork37@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.

Information we collect ourselves

None. Reef Rush does not have user accounts and does not send your gameplay, nickname or device information to any server operated by us.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings (sound, controls, visual effects), nickname, selected skin, personal best scores, tutorial progress, ad pacing counters and whether you bought Remove Ads. This stays on your device until you delete the app, unless you choose iCloud sync (below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Reef Rush uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google LLC, to show short ads between rounds and optional reward videos. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. To deliver, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

InformationUsed for
Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow trackingAd delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and personalized ads where permitted
IP address, which can indicate an approximate locationChoosing relevant and legally permitted ads, fraud prevention
Ad interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped and videos watchedMeasurement, reporting and ad performance
Diagnostic and performance informationKeeping the ad SDK working reliably

Google processes this information under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): where required, Google's consent message appears the first time you open the game. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads, purchases & privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • App Tracking Transparency: iOS asks whether Reef Rush may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, Google does not receive your advertising identifier and ads are not personalized using it. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase turns off between-round ads.

Your choices never change how the game plays.

In-app purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether the purchase is active.

Game Center and iCloud (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, Reef Rush uses Apple's Game Center to show your alias, submit personal best scores to leaderboards and report achievement progress. If you tap Sync profile, your selected skin, personal best scores and Ancient Ocean unlock are saved to your iCloud account through Game Center saved games. The saved file's name is derived from a one-way hash of your Game Center player ID. These services are operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive this data.

Nearby multiplayer (optional)

When you host or join a nearby game, Reef Rush uses Bluetooth or your local Wi-Fi network (Apple's Multipeer Connectivity) to connect directly to your friend's device. Only the information needed to play (the nickname you entered, your skin, controls and game state) is exchanged, and only with the devices in the game. It is not sent to us. iOS asks for Bluetooth or Local Network permission only when you use this feature. Solo play works without it.

Children

Reef Rush is rated 13+ on the App Store and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are restricted to general-audience content. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. Because we hold no personal information about you, most requests are best exercised through the ad choices above or directly with Google. We are happy to help: email norfork37@gmail.com. You can also reset your advertising identifier in iOS settings, or delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.

Security and retention

Nearby connections use encryption provided by iOS. We do not retain personal information. Google and Apple retain information under their own policies.

International transfers

Google and Apple may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
