Privacy Policy

App: Reef Rush (iOS)
Effective date: September 7, 2026
Contact: norfork37@gmail.com

Summary

Reef Rush does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases in the current version. We do not sell personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal data through Reef Rush for advertising, analytics, or cloud accounts.

  • On-device preferences: settings such as mute or camera zoom may be stored locally on your device.
  • Nearby multiplayer (optional): if you use Host/Join nearby, the app uses Apple’s local network / Multipeer Connectivity to exchange game state (for example player names you enter, inputs, and world state) directly between devices. This traffic is peer-to-peer on the local network and is not uploaded to our servers. We do not operate an online matchmaking service.

Permissions

Reef Rush may request Local Network access only so you can play with a nearby friend. Solo play works without granting this permission.

Children

Reef Rush is designed as a general-audience cartoon game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

The current App Store build does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated.
