Playing

How do I swim, dash and attack?

Touch anywhere on the left half of the screen and drag to swim. Dash, powers, split, caviar and Battle Royale shots are on the right. In Settings you can switch to Cruise steering (steer, then lift your thumb), shorter thumb travel, inverted movement, automatic battle shots and reduced motion.

What are the modes?

Coral Wilds (endless survival), Wreck Dive (hunt five divers as a whale), Current Run (race through gates), Moon Garden (peaceful, no predators), Kelp Nursery (90-second growth sprint), Storm Rush (60-second score chase), Crystal Caverns (reach whale size in five minutes) and Battle Royale (bubble combat with four teams). Reach 300,000 mass to unlock the Ancient Ocean: pause and choose Teleport to Ancient Ocean.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Solo play, and nearby play over Bluetooth, work fully offline. Ads, Game Center and iCloud sync need a connection.

Nearby multiplayer

How do I play with a friend?

Install the same version of Reef Rush on both iPhones or iPads. Choose the same mode, tap Play with nearby friends, choose the same connection type on both devices, then tap Host on one device and Join on the other. Keep both apps open.

Bluetooth or Nearby Wi-Fi?

Bluetooth connects two players and works with Wi-Fi turned off. Nearby Wi-Fi supports Battle Royale with up to eight devices, which must be on the same network. Guest or hotel networks sometimes block device-to-device traffic.

My friend's game doesn't appear.

Check that iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth and Local Network are allowed for Reef Rush, that Bluetooth is on and that both apps are in the foreground. Then go back to the menu and host or join again.

Ads and purchases

When do ads appear?

Only between finished solo rounds, never during a dive, a tutorial or a multiplayer game, and not in your first few rounds. After being eaten you can choose to watch a video to revive, once per dive.

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings → Ads, purchases & privacy → Remove ads. It's a one-time purchase that removes between-round ads and makes revives free.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads, or I have a new device.

Tap Settings → Ads, purchases & privacy → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If your region requires it, Ad privacy choices appears in the same Settings section. Tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Progress, Game Center and iCloud

Where is my progress saved?

Your skin, personal bests and Ancient Ocean unlock save on your device automatically. If you're signed in to Game Center, scores and achievements upload automatically. Tap Profile → Sync profile to back up your profile to iCloud and restore it on another device.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. Profiles saved to iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
